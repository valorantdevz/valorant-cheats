> This is almost always caused by an outdated Valorant Cheats build conflicting with a new Valorant patch. Update Valorant Cheats to the latest version. Also check that your antivirus (Windows) or Gatekeeper (macOS) isn't interfering mid-session — add `C:\ValorantCheats\` to your exclusion list or grant full disk access in System Settings.
